# Blinkit-Sales-Customer-Analysis
This repository contains a comprehensive sales analysis of Blinkit, focusing on key performance indicators (KPIs) such as total sales, average sales, item categories, and customer ratings. The analysis includes various visualizations to uncover insights about product performance, outlet efficiency, and sales trends over time.




# 🚀 Blinkit Sales & Customer Analysis

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. **Total Sales** – Overall revenue generated from all items sold.
2. **Average Sales** – The average revenue per sale.
3. **Number of Items** – Total count of different items sold.
4. **Average Rating** – The average customer rating for items sold.

---

## 📈 Chart Analysis & Insights

### 1️⃣ Total Sales by Fat Content
- **Objective:** Analyze how fat content impacts total sales.
- **Chart Type:** Donut Chart
- **Insights:**
  - Regular fat products contribute **65%** of total sales.
  - Low-fat products account for **35%**.

### 2️⃣ Total Sales by Item Type
- **Objective:** Identify which product categories generate the most revenue.
- **Chart Type:** Bar Chart
- **Insights:**
  - **Top-selling categories:**
    - Fruits & Vegetables (**$178.1K**)
    - Snack Foods (**$175.4K**)
    - Household (**$136.0K**)
  - **Low-performing categories:**
    - Seafood (**$9.1K**)
    - Breakfast (**$15.6K**)

### 3️⃣ Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales
- **Objective:** Compare sales distribution based on fat content across outlets.
- **Chart Type:** Stacked Column Chart
- **Insights:**
  - **Tier 3 outlets** have the highest sales for both low-fat and regular-fat products.
  - **Tier 1 outlets** generate the lowest revenue in both categories.

### 4️⃣ Total Sales by Outlet Establishment
- **Objective:** Assess how outlet establishment age/type affects sales.
- **Chart Type:** Line Chart
- **Insights:**
  - Sales **peaked in 2018 ($204.5K)** and declined slightly afterward.
  - Steady performance from **2011 to 2022**.

### 5️⃣ Sales by Outlet Size
- **Objective:** Determine the impact of outlet size on total revenue.
- **Chart Type:** Donut/Pie Chart
- **Insights:**
  - **Large outlets dominate sales** (**$507.9K, 42%**).
  - **Medium outlets contribute 37%** (**$444.8K**).
  - **Small outlets have the lowest sales** (**21%, $249.0K**).

### 6️⃣ Sales by Outlet Location
- **Objective:** Identify revenue distribution across locations.
- **Chart Type:** Funnel Map
- **Insights:**
  - **Tier 3 locations have the highest sales** (**$472.1K**).
  - **Tier 1 locations report the lowest revenue** (**$336.4K**).

### 7️⃣ All Metrics by Outlet Type
- **Objective:** Provide an overview of sales performance by different store types.
- **Chart Type:** Matrix Card
- **Insights:**
  - **Supermarket Type 1 generates the highest revenue** (**$787.5K**).
  - **Grocery stores have the lowest sales** (**$151.9K**).
  - **Supermarket Type 2 and Type 3 contribute similar sales** (~**$130K**).

## 🔍 Conclusions & Business Recommendations

### 1️⃣ High-Selling Products
✅ **Focus marketing efforts** on **fruits, snacks, and household products** to maximize revenue.
✅ **Improve promotions** for low-performing categories like **seafood and breakfast**.

### 2️⃣ Outlet Strategy
✅ **Expand and optimize large outlets**, as they drive the most revenue.
✅ **Invest more in Tier 3 locations**, as they outperform others.

### 3️⃣ Fat Content Trends
✅ **Regular fat items generate more revenue**, but low-fat products still hold a **35% market share**.
✅ **Target health-conscious consumers** by increasing visibility of **low-fat products**.

### 4️⃣ Sales Trends Over Time
✅ **Peak sales in 2018** suggest an opportunity to revisit past successful strategies.
✅ **Implement customer engagement programs** to sustain consistent sales over the years.
